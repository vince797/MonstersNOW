Why we built it

The drawing stays at the heart of the story.

Children already create characters with personalities, quirks, and stories of their own. MonstersNOW helps families preserve that spark by carrying the drawing's recognizable details into a polished storybook character.

Families can start free, review the result, and decide whether they want to turn that character into a printed keepsake.

What matters to us

Made for children. Clear for parents.

Their idea comes first

The child's drawing—not a stock character—is the creative starting point.

Preview before purchase

Families see the monster and approve the book before anything is printed.

Private by default

Artwork is never added to the public gallery without parent or guardian permission.
