Their story

From neighborhood duty to monster duty.

2 young founders + ∞ silly ideas

James and Luke aren't your typical business founders. They would usually rather be drawing, laughing, or dreaming up the next wonderfully weird idea—and that makes them exactly the right people to build a company about monster stories.

When the pencils are down, they are on neighborhood duty: scooping poop and keeping local lawns clean. It is honest work, even if the monsters they draw are a lot more fun to clean up after.

With big imaginations and an even bigger sense of humor, James and Luke help make MonstersNOW a place where every kid's idea can grow into a character, a story, and an adventure worth remembering.

↳ Their official job description? Make cool stuff, keep it fun, and never underestimate a kid with a crayon.
