Their story

Two kids who love to draw.

James and Luke are the brothers behind MonstersNOW. Their big imaginations and sense of humor shape every monster adventure.

When the pencils are down, they are on neighborhood duty: scooping poop and keeping local lawns clean. It is honest work, even if the monsters they draw are a lot more fun to clean up after.

They help make MonstersNOW a place where every kid's drawing can grow into a character, a story, and an adventure worth remembering.

For families

Families can create a monster for free, preview it before buying, and keep artwork private by default. The magic belongs to the kids; the important decisions stay with their grown-ups.
