Privacy and artwork data use

A plain-language explanation of what happens when an adult uploads a child’s drawing to MonstersNOW.

Last updated October 1, 2026

Adult useWhat we collectAI processingStorage and sharingYour choicesContact

Adults submit the artwork

MonstersNOW is designed for parents, guardians, and other adults purchasing or creating on behalf of a child. Children should not create accounts, enter contact details, or upload files themselves. The submitting adult must have permission to use the artwork and any names entered.

Please upload the drawing only. Do not upload a photograph of a child, school information, an address, or other sensitive details.

What we collect

  • The drawing and generated character or coloring-page files.
  • Names and story personalization entered by the adult.
  • Email, payment confirmation, shipping, and order information needed to complete a purchase.
  • Basic technical information needed for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

Payment card information is handled by the checkout provider and is not stored directly by MonstersNOW.

How AI is used

The uploaded drawing is sent to OpenAI’s image API so MonstersNOW can validate the image and generate the selected storybook character and coloring page. The drawing is used to provide the requested transformation, not to make decisions about the child.

AI-generated images can be imperfect. The adult reviews the character and book proof before printing. MonstersNOW may also use service providers for hosting, storage, payments, email, and print fulfillment when those functions are needed.

Storage, books, and public sharing

Artwork and generated files may be retained with the submission or order so the adult can review a proof, complete fulfillment, receive support, or reuse an approved monster. Order records are retained as needed for transactions, tax, fraud prevention, and customer service.

Artwork is private by default. MonstersNOW does not place a drawing or generated monster in the public Gallery unless the adult affirmatively grants the applicable sharing permission. Permission to show a generated monster does not automatically grant permission to show the original drawing.

Your choices

An adult may ask us to correct contact information, withdraw Gallery permission, or delete artwork and generated files that we are not required to retain for an active order or legal record. Removing source files may make it impossible to reproduce or reorder the same customized product.

Email hello@monstersnow.com with the order or submission details needed to locate the record. We may verify the requester’s identity and authority before acting.

Questions or concerns

Contact hello@monstersnow.com. If our practices materially change, this page and its effective date will be updated.
