Generated artwork and proof approval

AI-generated previews may interpret details differently from the original drawing. Colors and printed output can also vary slightly between screens and physical books. Review the character, names, spelling, page layout, and final proof carefully. Approval authorizes production of that version.

Prices, taxes, and available shipping methods are shown before payment. Personalized books are printed on demand after the required proof and payment steps are complete. The shipping and replacement policy is part of these terms.
