# Halloween Monster Night - Illustrated Spread Review

All 14 narrative spreads are illustrated using the built-in image-generation workflow and the approved opening reference. This is a sample featuring Mia and Moxie. Text will be composed separately, not generated into the artwork.

## Production status

- Selected artwork is listed below; v2 files supersede their v1 counterparts for review.
- Reviewed character identity, palette, story clues, warm kid-safe tone and binding clearance. Targeted corrections applied to pages 10-11, 12-13, 14-15 and 18-19.
- These integrated sample illustrations are not reusable character-free background plates. A personalized production workflow still needs validated monster replacement or scene regeneration.
- Assets are 1774 x 887 pixels, about 104 pixels per inch at a 17 x 8.5 inch spread. They are review resolution, not approved print resolution.
- Final typography, bleed, long-name testing, final bucket continuity, supporting-costume continuity and Lulu validation remain to be completed when assembling the full proof.
- No website deployment or Lulu submission has been performed.
