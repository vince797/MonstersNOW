Order expectations

Shipping, returns, and replacements

Personalized books are made for one child and one monster. Here is what to expect before and after production.

Last updated October 1, 2026

ProductionShippingChangesReturnsProblems

Proof first, then production

Your personalized book is not sent to print until the required proof and order checks are complete. Carefully review names, spelling, selected monster, format, and page artwork before approval. Each book is printed on demand rather than taken from existing inventory.

Shipping expectations

Available shipping methods, charges, and delivery estimates are shown during checkout or in the order confirmation. Delivery timing includes both print production and carrier transit. Estimates are not guarantees and can be affected by holidays, severe weather, address problems, carrier delays, or printer capacity.

Customers are responsible for entering a complete, accurate delivery address. Contact us promptly if an address needs correction; it may be too late to change after production or shipment begins.

Changes and cancellations

Contact hello@monstersnow.com as soon as possible. We will try to help before production begins. Because each item is customized, changes or cancellations generally cannot be made once the approved files have entered production.

Returns

Personalized products generally cannot be returned for preference changes, customer-entered spelling, an approved design choice, or other details shown in the accepted proof. This does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect books

If the book arrives damaged, has a manufacturing defect, or does not match the approved production file, email us promptly with the order number and clear photographs of the book, packaging, and shipping label. We will review the issue and, when eligible, arrange a replacement or another appropriate resolution.

Keep the book and packaging until the claim is resolved because the printer or carrier may require additional photographs or inspection.
