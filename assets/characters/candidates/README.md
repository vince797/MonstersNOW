# Unapproved Halloween character candidates

The eight canonical appearance IDs now have transparent, style-matched review candidates. They are not production art and have no human approval. Curly dark has four poses; the other seven have standing art only. The catalog is the runtime authority for paths, stable identities, pose availability and baseline anchors. Individual provenance JSON files retain exact source hashes and dimensions.

Deep coils uses `deep-coils-black-v1/standing-v2.png`; the earlier local standing.png is superseded and is not part of the selected review package. Quiet garden is an additional Curly dark emotion variant, not a replacement for the hopeful garden pose. The two-treat prop lives separately under assets/props/candidates/home-two-treats-v1.

The original generated PNGs are preserved. No background plates or active manuscript text were changed. Missing walking/seated poses, contact shadows, foreground masks, source print detail and human art approval remain release gates.
