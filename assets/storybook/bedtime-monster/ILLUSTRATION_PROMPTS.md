# Bedtime Monster - starter background plates

These are the first review assets for interior visual development. They were generated with the built-in image-generation tool as reusable 2:1 background plates. The starring child and personalized monster are intentionally absent and must be composited later from approved order assets.

The scenes establish a premium tactile soft-3D world of lavender fabrics, rounded wood, soft moonlight, and warm amber bedside light. They are concept-stage spreads until the manuscript and complete spread plan are approved.
