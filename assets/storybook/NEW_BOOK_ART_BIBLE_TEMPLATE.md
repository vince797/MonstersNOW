# MonstersNOW new-book visual development template

Use this art bible before producing a new personalized book. Complete it with the approved story manuscript, cover direction, and brand references in hand. The result is the visual contract for concept work, background-plate generation, Admin review, and print production.

- **Story promise:** One sentence describing the emotional experience for the child.
- **Audience / reading moment:**
- **Emotional arc:** Opening feeling -> midpoint change -> ending feeling.
- **Personalized roles:** What the child and monster contribute without assuming anatomy, clothing, powers, or specific physical actions.
- **Non-negotiable story symbols:** Define exact shape, color, scale, and behavior for any recurring object.

## 2. Visual concept

- **Visual thesis:** One sentence describing the book's world and emotional look.
- **Style / medium:** Default to premium tactile soft-3D children's-book illustration unless another direction is approved.
- **Shape language:** Rounded, angular, tall, low, clustered, open, etc.
- **Material language:** Fabrics, wood, glass, foliage, paper, stone, clouds, water, and other recurring textures.
- **Scale language:** How spaces should feel relative to the personalized characters.
- **Avoid list:** Franchises, scary imagery, anatomy-specific staging, generated lettering, and any book-specific exclusions.

## 5. Character and continuity rules

The background plate must not contain the starring child, the personalized monster, a demo creature, a silhouette, an outline, or a visible placement guide.

- **Child continuity:** Clothing and appearance are supplied by the approved order assets, not painted into reusable backgrounds.
- **Monster continuity:** The uploaded drawing and approved transformed monster remain the source of truth.
- **Supporting-character continuity:** Record name, silhouette, clothing, palette, scale, and first appearance for every fixed supporting character.
- **Prop continuity:** Track the page where each important prop appears, changes state, or disappears.
- **Environment continuity:** Track time of day, weather, light direction, and location transitions.

## 6. Spread plan

Plan the whole book before final art. Use one row per spread.

Spread rules:

- Treat the art as one 2:1 composition made of two facing square pages.
- Protect the central 10% gutter from faces, clues, important props, and placement zones.
- Review the full spread and each square-page crop.
- Keep copy-safe areas calm enough for final typography.
- Stage the child and monster through reserved space and light, never through baked-in placeholders.
- Avoid actions that require a particular number of limbs, a specific grip, clothing, wings, horns, or other anatomy.

## 7. Background-plate production prompt

Start every spread prompt with the book-specific visual direction, then include this rule:

> Create a reusable environment plate only. Do not render the personalized child, personalized monster, any substitute character, silhouette, outline, visible placeholder, or placement guide. Keep the specified child and monster zones clear, grounded, and naturally lit for later compositing. No generated lettering, logos, trademarks, or watermark.

Each final prompt must also name the spread pages, story beat, environment, style, composition, lighting, palette, text-safe area, gutter protection, and exact character-zone positions.

## 8. Review gates

### Gate A - concept approval

- Story promise, visual thesis, palette, and environment language support the manuscript.
- Cover direction and interior direction belong to the same visual world.
- Recurring symbols and props have explicit continuity rules.

### Gate B - spread-plan approval

- Every story beat has a planned spread.
- Page turns, quiet moments, reveals, and ending rhythm are intentional.
- Child, monster, text, and gutter zones are compatible on every spread.

### Gate C - background-art approval

- No personalized or substitute character is baked into the art.
- Ground, perspective, headroom, and light support later compositing.
- Important faces, clues, props, and story symbols remain outside the gutter and character zones.
- Adjacent spreads maintain supporting characters, props, lighting, and geography.
- No generated text, watermark, frightening imagery, or unapproved franchise element appears.

### Gate D - personalized proof approval

- Approved child and monster assets match the reserved zones and light direction.
- Neither character covers story copy, faces, clues, props, or the gutter.
- The full spread and both square-page crops read clearly.
- The personalized proof is approved in Admin before print output.

### Gate E - print readiness

- Final pagination, bleed, trim, resolution, color, cover dimensions, and vendor validation pass.
- Production status is updated with exact assets and approvals.
- No draft or review-only asset is published automatically.
