# Halloween Monster Night cover concepts

These square images are reusable front-cover background plates for the 8.5 x 8.5 inch book:

- `cover-title-scene-cinematic-muted-v4.png` — selected catalog-cover source. It matches the book's soft 3D neighborhood while retaining the collection's cream title field, purple lettering, and restrained original palette. The exact brand logo is composited separately inside the protected cover area by `scripts/build-catalog-covers.js`.
- `background-minimal-halloween-v3.png` — selected series direction; simple thematic background keeps the personalized monster and title dominant.
- `background-porch-gate-v1.png` — selected catalog direction; clearest foreground for a replaceable monster at thumbnail size.
- `background-town-square-v1.png` — broader parade-world alternative.
- `background-garden-arch-v1.png` — more magical, ornate alternative.
- `demo-monster-cutout-v1.png` — catalog-only demonstration character. Customer books must replace it with the selected generated monster.

The reusable background plates intentionally contain no title or character. The selected web catalog source includes the approved title treatment but still leaves the lower-center foreground available for a personalized monster in the final production cover.

The source plates are 1254 x 1254 pixels. The series builder creates a 2625 x 2625 front-panel candidate for 8.5 inch trim plus 0.125 inch bleed on all sides. Build the complete Lulu cover only after requesting the exact template/dimensions for the selected binding, paper, and 32-page count; the upload must be a one-page back-spine-front PDF.

Keep title, names, the star, faces, and the personalized monster inside the applicable safety margin. Hardcover casewrap needs substantially more protected space than paperback because of wrap and hinge areas.

## Series system

Every MonstersNOW cover should keep the same hierarchy: the small exact brand mark, high-contrast title, theme-specific full-bleed background, and a clear protected area for the personalized monster where the story calls for one. Preserve the brand scale and protected placement so the books read as one series and remain resilient to Lulu's trim, wrap, and hinge variance.
